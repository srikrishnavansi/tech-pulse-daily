shadcn-ui/cn

Drop-in replacement for tailwind-merge + clsx: same API, full parity, benchmarked at 30x faster (37x geomean across 58 real repos, per the maintainers). Zero dependencies, runs in React/Vue/Svelte/Astro/plain templates and in browser/Node/Bun/Deno/edge. `npx shadcn migrate cn` swaps it in with one command.

Why it matters: If RealGenie's or ReBuddy's frontends lean on Tailwind, this is a free win in exactly the render-heavy, component-dense screens where clsx/tailwind-merge overhead actually shows up — and it's cheap to try and cheap to revert.

OpenAI Agents API

Hosted API giving direct access to the Codex harness: OpenAI manages sessions, orchestration, context compaction and recovery; you supply tools and an execution environment (their sandbox or your own). Built around four primitives — Agent, Environment, Session, and Events/items.

It's a packaged version of the orchestration plumbing you're hand-rolling for the 13 RealGenie agents. Worth reading even if you don't switch, just to see where OpenAI drew the session/recovery boundaries versus your own harness.

macOS app that pins live usage-limit rings for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Antigravity to a screen edge, refreshing every 60s. Handles multiple accounts (e.g. a separate CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR work profile) as distinct rings.

Aimed squarely at the thing that actually derails an agent-heavy workday: not knowing you're about to hit a quota mid-task, across three different CLIs at once.

launch

MultiMatte (Feyn)

Promptable background removal — name the object in text, it returns an alpha matte (not a binary mask) for just that object, handling fine detail like hair. A LoRA fine-tune on SAM 3, touching 19.49M of its 860M params (2.27%); Feyn's own numbers put it at 0.901 S-measure vs SAM 3's 0.667 on DIS-VD, their benchmark, not independently verified.

Worth a look anywhere listing photos get processed — cleaner cutouts than mask-based removal, and small enough as a LoRA that self-hosting instead of a per-image API call is realistic.

Codex CLI drops the `codex mcp-server` entry point

The deprecated `codex mcp-server` command is gone in this release. Also ships GPT-6-Astra in the model picker and Bedrock catalog, plus experimental --worktree/session support for isolated checkouts.

If anything in your stack still shells out to `codex mcp-server`, this upgrade breaks it silently — check before you bump the version.

Skip this one

openai/NavierStokesAndEuler

OpenAI says roughly 1,000 AI agents helped produce Lean 4-verified proofs of finite-time blowup for Navier-Stokes and Euler, widely reported as "AI solved a Millennium Prize problem."

The Lean certificates do check out mechanically — it's a real, runnable repo, already at 1,731 stars — but that's not the same as the result being settled or original. Mathematicians close to the work are disputing priority against earlier human results (Buckmaster, Alpöge), and one contributor described one of the three companion papers as "AI slop." The formalization is real; the framing around it is the part that hasn't survived contact with the math community.
